## Skills worth automating

A DJ uses a cue or pre-listen to pick the moment, brings in the next groove, manages competing frequencies and decides when to give the new track the lead. Phrasing, short cuts, single-phrase blends, bass handovers and filter fades all serve distinct situations. The criteria below are proposals for Mixworks to calibrate by listening. [Native Instruments transition guide](https://www.native-instruments.com/blogs/blog/dj-transitions) · [AlphaTheta mixing guide](https://alphatheta.com/en/landing/dj-guide/)

| Skill | Evidence and treatment |
| --- | --- |
| **Short, beatmatched fader blend** | Implement first for reliable constant-tempo 4/4 grids and compatible tracks with usable overlap. Test 4- and 8-bar windows, starting from a credible change or an aligned bar when phrasing is uncertain. Use local level and available headroom to shape the fade. A linear curve can dip with unrelated signals and equal-power curves can swell with correlated signals. Judge the prepared sum. |
| **Bass handover during a fader blend** | Use when both sources carry measurable low-end weight in the actual overlap. Ease in the incoming percussion and upper range with the incoming bass reduced. Transfer low-end ownership at a supported boundary while the volume fades continue. Current band controls can express this. The existing activity profile supplies coarse bass evidence. Use finer local measurements before trusting a specific beat. |
| **Long progressive EQ blend or gentle filter fade** | Pilot with stable, sparse passages and credible phrase starts. Test 16 bars when the material supports it. Use filters selectively when they make room for an audible incoming layer. A change in RMS can suggest a place to inspect. Claims about vocals and hooks require additional evidence. |
| **Downbeat cut or drop swap** | Keep the explicit cut style. For automatic use require a reviewed cue pair or separately validated evidence for both an outgoing resolution and incoming impact, plus reliable timing and continuity across the switch. A matched grid or a rise in energy alone is insufficient evidence. |
| **Vocal-safe handover or percussion/stem bridge** | Use reviewed regions or aligned, ready stems with acceptable leakage. A validated vocal-presence or lead detector can broaden coverage later. Overlapping vocal hooks and stem leakage need listening fixtures. |
| **Echo-out or loop bridge** | Reserve for material with a suitable exit or a verified clean loop. Echo-out needs an owned, bounded tail that survives rendering and reopen. Loop extension needs reliable loop boundaries, continuity and source-clock checks. These are later skills. |

## First checkpoint

Make gradual fader blends the default for Balanced, Bold and DJ set (varied). Compare a small, bounded set of viable windows for each shortlisted pair and choose the timing and treatment together. Use retained grids and activity for the shortlist. Use local level, bass weight, silence and boundary evidence for the actual windows, with extra analysis limited to finalists where needed. Give missing phrase evidence a short, conservative blend where the grids and usable overlap support it. Try another window or partner when the overlap is poor. Keep generated automation editable and report the actual audible duration, technique and reason for each join. For a chain, leave enough room for the middle track to take the lead between handovers.

Use the same two sources and gain-matched playback for the current cut, a short fader blend and a bass handover. Start with the reported pair if the saved join can be identified, then cover sparse intros/outros, two heavy low ends, competing hooks and a short middle track. Listen before, through and after each join. The incoming groove should become audible before the outgoing one leaves, the bass should feel continuous, and the handover should preserve the musical sentence. Focused render checks should measure active overlap, headroom and a gap or level jump, and confirm editable nodes and save/reopen. Listening establishes whether those measurements translate into a convincing transition.
