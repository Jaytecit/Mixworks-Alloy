# Timeline tools audit — 5 November 2026

**Retire Edit details as a permanent tab once its precision tools have direct entry points. Keep Transitions as an optional, dockable pair editor.** Someone cutting a phrase or applying a preset should be able to stay on the waveform. Someone tuning several bands or rehearsing a blend benefits from controls that stay visible.

| **Fades & timing** | Route **Segment settings…** and `Ctrl+T` to a compact, named segment inspector with Start, Source in, Length, Fade in/out and Segment gain. Keep direct move and trim. Exact range, fixed selection lengths and automation owner selection still depend on the tab. Move those entry points before hiding it. Clicking Fades & timing in the tab clears a range or group, while invoking Segment Settings from a range menu is refused. A replacement should name the target and make the scope predictable. In the 1920×1080 capture, Add, Remove and Defaults occupy the top rows, while band details, Filter and Rehearsal require scrolling. Give the pair name, range, treatment and comparison controls priority. Let the editor stay closed after an ordinary frame selection or drag unless the user pins it open. Selecting a frame currently opens the console even when it was collapsed. ## Make the menu reflect the target

1. **Separate frame, segment, highlighted range/group, empty lane and node menus.** The captured frame right-click opened the segment menu. The range menu correctly named the time range, then listed more than 30 entries, including both **Delete this piece** and **Delete selected audio**. It also showed enabled segment settings and all-lane actions that the dispatch path refuses for ranges/groups. Lead with the highlighted scope and leave other scopes to their own menus. Keep the existing warnings for unlinking and changes across lanes. [Timeline menu and dispatch](../Source/TimelineEditing.cpp#L48) · [Frame hit testing](../Source/Workstation.cpp#L1201)
2. **Make pointer time and playhead time explicit.** Split and Paste from the timeline use the clicked position. The panel and keyboard routes use the playhead. Preserve both and label them accordingly. Bind the frame menu to its pair even when a track is part of another transition. Preserve the playhead and musical state when opening or dismissing a menu or inspector. Recheck the target and its locks before editing, including after confirmation.
3. **Keep a visible and keyboard route.** Show **Edit selection…** when audio is highlighted and **Edit transition…** on the selected frame. Offer the same context from the focused target via the Menu key or Shift+F10. Keep relevant Edit/View entries and readable reasons for actions temporarily unavailable. [Windows menus and context menus](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/develop/ui/controls/menus-and-context-menus)

## First checkpoint

Prototype the range and frame menus with the current panels available. Verify that a user can drag-select and copy a phrase, paste at an explicit destination, and Undo from the timeline. Verify that they can apply a preset to one named pair, open its editor for a band adjustment and compare A/B/C without repeatedly opening menus. Cover overlapping pairs, compact tracks, locks, selection changes and keyboard access before removing the permanent Edit details tab.
