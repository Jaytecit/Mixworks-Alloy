# Alloy visual direction

Current artistic direction, reconciled 4 November 2026. This document owns artistic direction;
[HANDOVER](../HANDOVER.md) owns the current candidate and evidence. Earlier proof
milestones and catalogue targets are historical, not instructions to restart work.

## Experience

The 5 November [Originals refinement plan](ORIGINAL-SCENE-REFINEMENT-20261105.md)
reserves large audio-driven movement for detected kicks and keeps smaller
continuous reactions for the rest of the music. It also records the shared
purple grade and rough-surface findings and defines the four-scene preview.

Build rich music-driven scenes with substantial forms, depth, readable imagery,
strong colour variety and motion that remains interesting between impacts.
Quiet and energetic passages should visibly differ in movement, not just light.
Bass can drive depth/expansion, mids turns/bends and treble local articulation;
use integrated phases and momentum where release should slow rather than reverse
movement. A numerical pixel difference is not proof of musical quality.

Prefer solid/geometric compositions in the direction of Dimensional Pulse over a
particle-heavy show. Keep the retained shortlist. Variants, mirrors/kaleidoscopes,
image choices and distinct palettes are welcome; disclose shared mechanisms and
do not count named looks as independent scenes. The active library currently has
204 scenes: 203 with six executable looks plus Jumping Techno Tunnel. Jay accepted the five examples
and authorised the full eighty additions, now [implemented and ready for review](VISUAL-EIGHTY-20261103.md).
The [catalogue](IMAGE-INSTRUMENTS.md) records ten families of eight.

## Images and materials

Complete, readable images should appear frequently. Pump the complete image to
the beat and apply different motion/effects in different scenes. Nested charts
and fragmentation remain useful choices, not universal defaults.

Mosaic-style materials may repeat a whole image in each tile. Selected scenes
and looks should instead show a complete image behind the tiles, visible through
gaps and refracted/distorted by see-through tile faces. The image must read as a
continuous source rather than unrelated pieces pasted over individual facets.
Mosaic Kiln and Spinning Stamps offer Glass image; Rhombus Foundry and Enamel Scales
use that continuous-image treatment by default.

Particle music response must affect trajectories and collective patterns, not
only each particle's brightness or size. Give particle scenes distinctive scenery
and effects, and periodically resolve the particles into the complete source image.
Travel cameras must preserve clearance through structures under strong input.

## Kaleidoscopes and colour

Jay's latest feedback requests selectable stronger palettes including bright neon,
less repetition of wavy surface veins across a mix, and particle trajectories
that continue through complete-image assembly without a full-image pause.
Palette changes alone do not count as new scenes. The expansion target is around
200 scenes, with about 80 additions departing from existing formulas. See the
[ten-family proposal](VISUAL-EXPANSION-203-PROPOSAL.md); its expansion is delivered. Remaining whole-show and artistic acceptance is W15
in [the roadmap](../FINISHING-PLAN.md), not another expansion instruction.

Jay subsequently supplied twenty preferred image-using Synesthesia references,
recorded in that proposal. Give image-driven generation greater priority when
refining the eighty additions: images can seed evolving systems and shape motion
or geometry as well as supply visible materials. Existing study associations do
not establish reference parity; compare characteristic moving behaviour using
matched inputs. This refines the expansion rather than cancelling its variety,
stronger palettes, readable-image moments or continuous particle assembly.

Use kaleidoscope treatments especially when a scene has less inherent musical
movement. Preserve complete-image readability where it is the composition's point;
reflected looks remain selectable. Provide MUCH more colour variation/recolouring
of actual image and scene pixels, both from musical attacks and continuous cycling.
Colour variation complements substantial motion rather than substituting for it.

## Transitions

The target is continuous musical movement through a join calculated from both
scenes' parameters. Start with compatible shape, motion and material models.
The sculpture and solid-instrument families each have a shared distance-field and
camera morph. Thirteen layered relief families share a height and material morph.
Other pairs still use feedback and need compatible parameter paths. See [implemented behaviour and
limitations](MILKDROP.md#transparent-sprites-and-generative-handoffs).
Music transition timing remains independently owned. Punchy pumps, snap rotations
and forward/back surges are accepted artistic tools; do not smooth away impact
merely because it jumps. Scene-specific motion and readable coverage matter more
than applying a generic background everywhere. The [60-scene energy review](SCENE-ENERGY-20261104.md)
is complete; future refinements need a specific observed shortcoming.

## Review and evidence

Use short soundtrack previews and inspect actual frames/native delivery. Check
image participation, musical path changes, pauses/seeks, both live transition
sides, and readable visual structure. Keep failed attempts and source/bundle
identities independently of Git. Respect the low-disruption verification policy.
Shared named controls must execute real behaviour; JSON prose is not routing.

The [library record](SCENE-LIBRARY-120-20261103.md),
[motion revision](VISUAL-MOTION-20261103.md) and
[glass/maintenance record](MAINTENANCE-20261103.md) retain dated implementation and
verification scope. No visual acceptance follows from scene counts or tests alone.
