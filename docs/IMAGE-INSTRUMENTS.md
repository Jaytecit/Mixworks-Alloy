# Image Instruments — eighty original scenes

Jay approved this expansion on 3 November 2026 after accepting the five example visuals. The collection contains ten instruments with eight authored compositions each. Together with the previous 123 entries, the active catalogue contains 203 scenes. Six palette looks per scene are separate treatments, not extra scenes.

## Behaviour and workflow

The selector provides overview, selected-image and live-mix views without development retention controls. Search matches scene names, styles and family names. Existing review files and curated registries are preserved on disk.

The automatic show balances scene usage and applies recent family/motif penalties over an eight-scene window, with a stronger adjacent-family penalty. Limited pools remain usable; particle scenes remain occasional accents. Palette selection stays independent of view/image methods.

Images seed populations, steer flocks/flows, shape surfaces or supply spatial materials and recurrent optical content. The collection includes chemical growth, Gaussian-neighbourhood cellular life, stored particle dynamics, image-height landscapes, persistent liquid surfaces, region glitches, advected pigment, implicit solids, spatial instruments and optical memory. The liquid and flow systems are bounded authored approximations, not full fluid solvers.

Development reference associations and final evidence are in the [expansion checkpoint](VISUAL-EIGHTY-20261103.md). No proprietary shader code, recovered host helpers or reference-program assets are part of these compositions. The existing attributed CC0 scene remains separately attributed.
