opaque source reveals them more clearly than the centred logo does. They are
not interchangeable copies, and the original five-scene concern does not justify
rewriting all five. The strongest remaining overlap is the horizontal trio:
Wake, Press and Fault. Even with different velocity fields, their broad retained
image and soft saturated bands remain close in appearance.

not measured optical flow or validated physical fluid simulation. Independent
musical-band causality was not tested in this comparison.
