Main feature shortlist
Competitor	Main features
DJ.Studio	Timeline mixing; automatic playlist ordering by BPM/key; phrase-aware transitions; editable transition presets; EQ/effects automation; stems; sample lanes; VST3/AU effects; DJ library integration; Ableton export; audio/video export. Features depend on edition. dj.studio
DropLab	Beat-synchronised timeline; transition presets; tempo, volume, EQ and effects curves; stem separation; pitch shifting/time stretching; slicing and looping; playlist rearrangement that preserves existing transitions; timeline notes; multiple project tabs; audio export. Create DJ mixes
GlideMix	Timeline mixing; beatgrid/phrase editing; harmonic matching and energy displays; tempo curves; transition effects; stems; VST3/AU effects; drawn/recorded automation; DJ library integration; separate headphone cue; live radio broadcasting; Ableton Link. Currently beta. glidemix.com
Kuartz	Timeline plus live decks; beatgrid/key/phrase analysis; track recommendations; automatic set building through an AI assistant; transition templates; recorded automation; real-time stems; effects and VST3/AU hosting; MIDI/HID mapping; headphone cueing; recording; desktop/mobile versions. kuartz.app
Mixxit	Manual timeline arrangement; BPM/key analysis; individual/global tempo adjustment; optional tempo matching for newly added tracks; volume/EQ automation; clip grouping, copying, markers and locking; WAV/MP3 export. Its help explicitly says tempo changes also change pitch, and tempo matching does not automatically align beats or phrases. mixxit.app


Visuals and music-reactive features

Here, “visuals” means audience-facing graphics—not waveforms, meters or coloured beatgrids.
Competitor	What I could confirm
DJ.Studio	Substantial built-in visualiser and video editor. Audio-reactive animations; beat-synchronised shader backgrounds; reactive spectrum displays; waveform overlay; particles; imported images/MP4 backgrounds; album artwork; track information; custom text/logo overlays; visual transitions; scheduled visual changes; video export. DJ.Studio Help Center
GlideMix	Synchronisation with external visual software. Ableton Link sends tempo, beat and play/stop to applications such as Resolume and TouchDesigner. I found no documented built-in audience visualiser or video exporter. Link supplies timing; the external application creates the visuals. glidemix.com
Kuartz	No audience-facing reactive visualiser or video exporter found in the published features/help reviewed. Its documented strengths concern audio, automation and performance controls. kuartz.app
DropLab	No audience-facing visualiser, reactive scene system or video exporter found in the published feature list reviewed. Create DJ mixes
Mixxit	No audience-facing visualiser, reactive scene system or video exporter found in the published features/help reviewed. mixxit.app


DJ.Studio’s visuals are more developed than my earlier answer conveyed. However, its current documentation says custom Shadertoys can no longer be imported by URL; users select from its built-in library. I also found no confirmation of an unrestricted scene-building system with your proposed nested sprites, arbitrary audio mappings and custom 2D/3D scenes. DJ.Studio Help Center

Why none is a confirmed “everything” product
Candidate	What prevents it covering the combined shortlist
DJ.Studio	Explicitly does not support MIDI controllers, and says live deck mixing is outside its plans. Its reactive visuals therefore don’t come with Kuartz-style hardware performance. DJ.Studio Help Center
GlideMix	Offers live timeline playback, cueing and external visual synchronisation, but explicitly says controller knobs and jog wheels aren’t mapped. Help
Kuartz	Combines extensive mixing and hardware performance features, but I couldn’t confirm DJ.Studio-style integrated visual creation/video export.
DropLab / Mixxit	Their documented feature sets cover fewer of the advanced performance, plug-in and visual capabilities above.


For Alloy, the potential distinction is the combination: detailed timeline editing, intelligent arrangement, a properly mapped live desk, and an integrated, customisable reactive visual system. The research supports that as a possible gap among these five; it does not establish that no other product addresses it.
