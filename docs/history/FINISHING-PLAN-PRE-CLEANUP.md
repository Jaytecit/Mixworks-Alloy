## The finish line

The proposed first finish line is a reliable mix-production release: import the library, check and correct musical timing, arrange tracks, see and protect transitions, mix with effects, recover work, and save/reopen/export a complete long mix. DJ.Studio is the primary transition-editing reference; retain the useful MixMeister workflows and Alloy appearance already chosen.

The complete wishlist also includes substantial new products inside the workstation: Smart DJ, stems, video, instruments, cloud integrations and dedicated hardware. They remain in the roadmap below. Completing the first release does not imply those requests have been fulfilled, or that MixMeister/DJ.Studio DSP parity has been established. This scope split is a recommendation, not a previously approved reduction of the user's wishes.
