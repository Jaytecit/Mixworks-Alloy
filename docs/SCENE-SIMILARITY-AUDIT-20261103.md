# Scene similarity audit — 3 November 2026

All **203 active scenes** were screened against the installed 0.55.68 images80 runtime. There are worthwhile overlaps to fix, including Jay's Afterhours Skyline / Dreamline Metropolis example. The largest common cause is **28 scenes defaulting to the same sixfold reflection finish**, which conceals otherwise different structures. Treat this as a prioritised comparison list, not a proposal to delete scenes or rewrite every participant.

## Evidence and limits

- Every scene directory matches the activated bundle byte for byte. Inventory, source hashes and an independent source ZIP are retained in `.local/scene-audit-20261103`.
- Fresh offscreen GPU renders cover every scene at current defaults, serially at 320×180: 76 frames each with the same owned artwork/music excerpt, retaining frames at 0.83, 1.67 and 2.50 seconds. All 203 current endpoint frames were visually inspected. Selected motion strips and prior matching-source family motion sheets were also inspected. Render acknowledgement checks succeeded for every scene.
- The older selector thumbnails were used only for initial triage. Several predate current shaders/defaults; they are not the authority for this verdict. Grayscale/edge correlation helped locate candidates but is not a perceptual duplicate score. Shared source is not automatically a duplicate: its active branches, topology, defaults and band responses were considered.
- Six additional plain-view controls and ten full-frame opaque-image/same-palette probes distinguish default reflection and source-content effects. They are temporary offscreen requests, not persisted scene edits. See the evidence below and the probe interpretation.
- This is a short-sample artistic audit, not an exhaustive test of all 1,218 looks, all user images, long feedback evolution, quiet/build/drop passages, native interactions, listening or display performance. Musical-response descriptions come from shader/control inspection and short rendered sequences; independent band-isolation testing was not performed.
- Single-context review, following Jay's instruction to avoid subagents/duplicate reviews. Impeccable design principles informed the comparison; a formal interface heuristics/detector audit was not applicable to this scene-content task. A brief skills.sh method check did not require additional tools or installation.
- No application source, defaults, presets, running session, launcher, user music or production data were changed. No build, regression, stress or capacity suite was run.

**Probe interpretation:** All six plain-view probes recovered clear woven, wave, contour, ribbon, root and paper structures from the hazy/web-like defaults. This supports changing the default finish before rewriting those families. The full-frame chart preserved the close liquid and raster relationships, so those are not solely a centred-logo artefact. It made Chroma Monsoon / Saffron Undertow / Chromatic Fault more visibly different (swirl, directional settling and shear respectively); keep the pigment group as a conditional refinement, not a proven duplicate family. Chromaflora / Coral Cipher also separate more clearly with the chart's edge structure. All sixteen requests completed successfully.

## How to read the findings

**Priority** means a strong current overlap or a common cause worth addressing first. **Refine** means related scenes whose intended difference should become clearer. **Watch** means intentional family resemblance or input-sensitive similarity; keep pending longer artistic comparison. **Distinct in sample** means no priority overlap found in this bounded review, not a guarantee across all settings. A scene may appear in more than one group; its appendix verdict uses the highest priority. Participants include useful anchors to preserve, not just scenes proposed for change.

The five approved examples — Chromaflora, Kinetic Bloom, Lucent Ranges, Mercurial Echo and Broken Spectrum — should remain anchors while their less differentiated siblings are refined. Bright shared palettes and continuous particle motion through assembly remain accepted requirements.

The appendix assigns **48 scenes to Priority, 23 to Refine, 47 to Watch and 85 to Distinct in sample**. These are editorial triage labels, not measured defect counts. The 48 include all 28 participants in the shared-default issue and retained reference anchors; they do not mean 48 independent rewrites are needed.

## First useful milestone

1. Restore a plain authored default for the affected sixfold scenes and compare them before rewriting geometry. Retain the radial treatment as a deliberate selectable look.
2. Distinguish the city pair by camera and skyline geometry; distinguish the three ribbon-feedback scenes by their emitted material and musical response.
3. Separate image stages and the glass pair through visible depth/occlusion and silhouette.
4. Refine the closest liquid/signal siblings while preserving the approved examples. Reassess pigment, landscape and particle groups after that small preview.

Success would be recognising each revised scene from silhouette and motion with the same source and palette, then seeing different, understandable responses to the same musical passage. No change is implemented by this audit.

### A01 — Priority: Sixfold defaults erase family identity

Current woven, ceramic, contour, ribbon, botanical, rain and paper outputs become overlapping radial webs or low-contrast rosettes. This is a shared presentation cause, not evidence that all 28 base shaders are duplicates.

**Direction:** Start by giving these scenes their plain authored view; reserve sixfold for an optional look or a few deliberate mandalas. Reassess the family pairs below afterward. Six representative offscreen plain-view probes accompany this audit; no defaults have been changed.

### A02 — Priority: Afterhours Skyline / Dreamline Metropolis

**Scenes:** Afterhours Skyline; Dreamline Metropolis.

Both use the same repeated box-city SDF, high forward-looking camera, window pattern and band mapping. VARIANT changes travel rate and palette/material phase, while control defaults change framing and finish. There is no separate city topology for the second scene.

**Direction:** Keep Afterhours as a distant, slow lateral skyline with a stable horizon and bass-driven window districts. Make Dreamline a lower street/canyon flight, taller irregular towers, mid-driven junction turns and treble-driven traffic. Camera height, skyline silhouette and musical action should distinguish them even in monochrome.

### A03 — Priority: Graphite Wake / Ember Advection / Opal Smoke

**Scenes:** Graphite Wake; Ember Advection; Opal Smoke.

All three select the same ribbon injection branch (VARIANT modulo 3 = 0) in the shared feedback shader. Current output repeats luminous wavy ribbons with similar advected history. Different flow frequency, colour and controls do not supply a sufficiently different subject.

**Direction:** Keep Graphite as a narrow calligraphic wake; give Ember short rising ember jets with bass-triggered emission and fast decay; give Opal broad translucent billows with slow mid-driven circulation. Prismatic Drift already uses a different noise-like injection branch and should remain the contrast anchor.

### A04 — Priority: Pulse Gallery / Orbit Theatre / Resonant Iris

**Scenes:** Pulse Gallery; Orbit Theatre; Resonant Iris.

The central intact image dominates all three. STAGE does supply square, oval and lobed frame geometry and different chart motion, but those differences live behind almost the same central subject. Their exposed defaults differ only in hue.

**Direction:** Keep Pulse Gallery as the frontal readable-image stage. Give Orbit a visibly off-axis moving image plane and independent satellites; make Iris a physical aperture whose leaves reveal and occlude the image. Drive depth pulses, orbital momentum and aperture opening respectively, rather than letting all three read as image pumping plus rings.

### A05 — Priority: Rhombus Foundry / Enamel Scales

**Scenes:** Rhombus Foundry; Enamel Scales.

Different tile masks become hard to read through the same .9 glass transmission and continuous source image. Both currently present an intact central image refracted through a dark faceted wall. Main geometry differs, so this is a material/framing collision rather than identical code.

**Direction:** Retain Rhombus as offset diamond glass panels with coherent lateral travel. Give Enamel large overlapping curved scales, an oblique grazing view, local opening on bass and a travelling mid-driven ripple. Keep transmission, but expose the silhouette and depth of each scale. Do not replace the recently requested continuous image with repeated logos.

### A06 — Priority: Paper Amphitheatre / Mountain Wings / Cloud Proscenium

**Scenes:** Paper Amphitheatre; Mountain Wings; Cloud Proscenium.

All use the same layered paper contour construction with smooth, absolute-sine or higher-frequency edges. Sixfold defaults additionally wash them into very similar hazy radial surfaces.

**Direction:** Restore plain view first. Then retain Amphitheatre as horizontal stage terraces, give Mountain Wings two separated angular hinged masses, and give Cloud broad disconnected floating layers with sky gaps. Use bass lift, mid hinge movement and slow treble erosion as different responses; reassess before rewriting all three.

### A07 — Priority: Mercurial Echo / Satin Vortex / Pressure Garden / Oilprint

**Scenes:** Mercurial Echo; Satin Vortex; Pressure Garden; Oilprint.

The shared damped height/velocity field and reflective source-art surface dominate. Satin Vortex and Oilprint also have identical exposed default controls; their internal forcing differs. Existing and refreshed samples still read as variants of the same embossed liquid image.

**Direction:** Preserve approved Mercurial Echo. Make Satin an unmistakable deep spiral with a visible centre; Pressure Garden four spatially separated impact sources; Oilprint a flatter thin-film surface with independent chromatic shear and treble shimmer. Strengthen flow shape and lighting before considering palette changes.

### A08 — Priority: Signal Collage repetition

**Scenes:** Broken Spectrum; Glass Interlace; Chromatic Splice; Afterimage Static; Raster Roulette; Circuit Confetti.

Broken Spectrum / Glass Interlace / Raster Roulette repeatedly show the same centred source with raster tears and channel splits. Afterimage Static / Circuit Confetti form a second close pair of mostly intact image with rectangular patches. Chromatic Splice has a more useful diagonal distinction, although the source silhouette still dominates.

**Direction:** Preserve approved Broken Spectrum. Make Glass Interlace visibly opposed temporal columns; Raster Roulette a sweeping rolling acquisition front; Afterimage Static a mosaic of older held blocks; Circuit Confetti sparse independently moving image islands. Keep Splice diagonal. Give each a different event/release rule instead of shared-looking channel flicker.

Chroma Monsoon, Iridescent Wake, Nebula Press, Saffron Undertow and Chromatic Fault have different vector forcing, but short image-led samples share a soft warped central source with warm colour. This is less literal than the city pair; full-frame source content and longer state evolution matter.

### A10 — Refine: Woven fabric / ribbon crossovers

**Scenes:** Bowed Tapestry; Ribbon Switchyard; Velvet Meanders; Overpass Weave.

Bowed Tapestry, Ribbon Switchyard, Velvet Meanders and Overpass Weave share dense curved bands and the same relief lighting/material language. Sixfold makes the crossover substantially worse.

**Direction:** After plain-view restoration, retain Tapestry as fine continuous cloth; make Switchyard sparse angular junctions with real crossings, Meanders broad isolated velvet strips, and Overpass strong depth-separated bridges. Prefer local tension, route switching and slow flexing to the same whole-field bend.

### A11 — Refine: Accordion Quay / Divided Sails

**Scenes:** Accordion Quay; Divided Sails.

Both read as repeated vertical folded/slatted fields with horizontal separators. The divided-side offset and mask frequency are real but relatively subtle.

**Direction:** Keep Accordion as continuous attached pleats. Open substantial sky gaps between independently hinged sails and alternate their direction on mids; let bass deepen the quay folds without moving the entire field.

### A12 — Refine: Lantern Orchard / Crown Procession / Glass Instruments

**Scenes:** Lantern Orchard; Crown Procession; Glass Instruments.

Regular arrays of glossy rounded objects give these scenes a similar first impression despite different family labels. Crown is somewhat more elongated; Glass Instruments does not yet make its optical role obvious with this source.

**Direction:** Keep Orchard as suspended staggered lights with hanging connections; make Crown a moving procession on a readable path; give Glass Instruments actual clear lenses with visible image refraction, mixed lens sizes and a slower optical response.

### A13 — Refine: Pendant Chains / Lenticular Hall

**Scenes:** Pendant Chains; Lenticular Hall.

Dense vertical elongated lens/tube arrays overlap in silhouette and lighting. The chains are segmented, but at show distance both can read as the same coloured curtain.

**Direction:** Expose hanging joints and pendulum motion in Chains. Make Lenticular Hall a deep perspective corridor of continuous lenses, with image changes tied to viewing angle rather than matching the pendulum movement.

### A14 — Refine: Shell Amphitheatre / Scalloped Basin / Radial Interchange

**Scenes:** Shell Amphitheatre; Scalloped Basin; Radial Interchange.

All can resolve into concentric scalloped ribbon rosettes. The last belongs to a different family but shares the dominant radial outline. Sixfold amplifies this collision.

**Direction:** Restore plain view, then separate an open half-shell stage, a concave bowl seen obliquely, and sparse road-like crossing ribbons with gaps and over/under depth. Give each a stable distinguishing silhouette.

### A15 — Refine: Coral Fans / Fern Conservatory / River Roots

**Scenes:** Coral Fans; Fern Conservatory; River Roots.

The botanical names share a narrow repeated root/branch-line construction. In current sixfold views they become very similar lace webs; plain-view source also differs mainly through offsets and axis choice.

**Direction:** Retain Coral as broad fans. Give Fern clear branching fronds with leaflets and uncurling growth; make River Roots asymmetric branching paths around open channels. Distinguish growth and propagation from cyclic line bending.

### A16 — Refine: Rain family

**Scenes:** Rain Pavilion; Cascade Staircase; Slanting Monsoon; Convergent Falls.

The same lane/drop construction is transformed by shorter drops, diagonal rotation or convergence. Distinct mathematical variants still use a common visual vocabulary; the shared sixfold view masks the directional differences.

**Direction:** First remove the default radial view. Preserve vertical rain, give Cascade a visibly stepped physical waterfall, keep Monsoon diagonal wind, and make Convergent Falls meet at a readable basin. Separate continuous fall speed from beat-triggered splash and cascade timing.

### A17 — Refine: Diamond Channels / Zigzag Sails

**Scenes:** Diamond Channels; Zigzag Sails.

Both show a dense grid of faceted diamond/pyramid units. Rotation and row shear provide variation, but the subject and response remain close.

**Direction:** Keep Diamond as a connected channel network. Make Sails long alternating open folded sheets with empty channels and local hinges; use mids for directional changes rather than the same faceted grid movement.

### A18 — Refine: Chromaflora / Coral Cipher

**Scenes:** Chromaflora; Coral Cipher.

Both maintain a similar source-shaped chemical labyrinth with nearly the same palette and relief. The edge-fed source mechanism is real but does not produce a strong enough visible difference in these short samples.

**Direction:** Preserve approved Chromaflora. Make Cipher a sparse contour skeleton with long crossing feeds, large gaps and treble-triggered edge propagation; avoid another dense filled organism.

### A19 — Refine: Lucent Ranges / Opaline Dunes / Lucid Escarpment

**Scenes:** Lucent Ranges; Opaline Dunes; Lucid Escarpment.

All use a forward-looking triangulated image terrain and a similar horizon. Their topology differs, but dune ridges and the asymmetric escarpment are not always clear at show distance.

**Direction:** Preserve approved Lucent Ranges. Give Dunes long smooth lateral ridges with wind travel and fewer hard facets; give Escarpment a dominant vertical wall and side-travelling camera. Keep the already clearer Ravine, Delta and overhead Canopy contrasts.

### A20 — Refine: Dense annular particle variants

**Scenes:** Plasma Seeds - Colour convection; Orbital Etching - Binary satellites; Overdrive Rings - Ring exchange; Star Nursery - Accretion disc.

Plasma Seeds - Colour convection, Orbital Etching - Binary satellites, Overdrive Rings - Ring exchange and Star Nursery - Accretion disc can all produce a dense annulus of the same image chips. Their force laws are different; they are not identical simulations.

**Direction:** Make convection spatially turbulent without a dominant ring, binary satellites clearly two centres, ring exchange two separated rings, and accretion a thin inclined disc with inward streams. Give each different particle density, depth and release timing; preserve continuous motion through assembly.

### A22 — Watch: New flock collection resemblance

**Scenes:** Kinetic Bloom; Ribbon Rookery; Aurora Couriers; Ion Ballet; Scarlet Relay; Spectral Migration; Halo Caravan; Prismatic Waltz.

Kinetic Bloom and its seven siblings share sprite rendering and persistent state. Magenta clusters can look similar, but row flocks, attraction poles, migration bands, filaments and spirals already supply visible differences.

**Direction:** Keep approved Kinetic Bloom. If further separation is needed, emphasise each existing trajectory: long ribbons, small couriers, separated poles, needle trails, broad migration, deep orbital bands and exchanging spirals. Verify a longer same-palette passage before selecting another rewrite.

### A23 — Watch: Repeated source-image composition across families

**Scenes:** Solar Wash; Afterimage Static; Circuit Confetti; Chromatic Salon; Velvet Apertures; Chroma Escalier; Catoptric Tide; Afterglow Atlas.

Several feedback, signal and optical scenes preserve or repeat an intact central image. A centred logo makes them look related even when their state and projection differ. Repeated-source layouts in Velvet Apertures and Chroma Escalier are an additional resemblance, not proof of the same algorithm.

**Direction:** Avoid presenting these consecutively with the same image. Strengthen room perspective, aperture occlusion, echo age or asymmetric reflection where the scene needs a clearer identity. Keep readable-image stages as an intentional part of the collection; do not make every scene fragment the source.

### A24 — Watch: Centred sculptural bodies

**Scenes:** Prism Bloom; Opal Reactor; Velvet Geode; Facet Voyager.

These share a centred round source-bearing object, but Prism Bloom has visible openings, Opal Reactor is a soft fused body, Velvet Geode is a cut shell and Facet Voyager is a hard polyhedral form. Current silhouettes are sufficiently distinct to avoid an immediate rewrite.

**Direction:** Keep their silhouettes. A future refinement could distinguish camera distance, shell translucency and local deformation; changing their colours alone would not add meaningful variety.

### A25 — Watch: Forward portal and tunnel vocabulary

**Scenes:** Neon Run; Cathedral Descent; Traced Arcade; Kinetic Vault; Honeylight Causeway.

Neon Run, Cathedral Descent, Traced Arcade, Kinetic Vault and Honeylight Causeway share forward depth, but outlined neon, architectural enclosure, triangles, curved ribs and hexagonal frames read differently in current samples.

**Direction:** Keep these as intentional contrasts. Avoid adjacent runs of tunnels; if needed, separate fixed camera/passing structures from camera flight and make music alter local geometry rather than universally accelerating every tunnel.
