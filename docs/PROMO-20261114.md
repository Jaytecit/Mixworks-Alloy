## Editorial approach

Show one understandable journey: choose music, arrange it, shape the handovers,
perform, and bring the mix to life visually. The guiding line is **“Your music.
Your mix. Your show.”** Music carries the film; concise captions identify benefits
and actual controls without turning it into a narrated feature inventory.

Use real, legible app views for workflow claims. Crop and scale around the action,
keep captions away from relevant controls, and allow the resulting change to read
before cutting. Preserve Alloy's copper/orange visual identity. Choose contrasting,
composed scenes with readable artwork, depth and motion; avoid an uninterrupted
sequence of similar tunnels or visually noisy flashes. Reserve substantial screen
time for moving visuals, the strongest immediate expression of the product.
