# Background images

Images in this folder supply environments, surface textures, distortions and
image effects. They are treated as backgrounds even if they contain alpha.
Sprites is also scanned: its transparent images become cutouts; opaque images
remain usable as backgrounds while you organise your collection.

Identical image content is included only once across both folders, preferring
Backgrounds. Copies are not deleted or moved. Reopen Visuals to refresh the list.
At most 512 unique images from the first 1024 candidates are considered.
